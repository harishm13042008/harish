## Empathy Map Canvas (Target User: Aspiring Comic Storyteller / Student / Educator)

| Section | Details |
|---|---|
| **Says** | "I have a great story but I can't draw." / "I wish I could make a comic quickly." / "Hiring an artist is too expensive." |
| **Thinks** | "Will AI keep my characters looking the same in every panel?" / "Is the story good enough to share?" / "I need this ready for my class or page soon." |
| **Does** | Writes story ideas in notes; searches for comic makers; tries free templates; asks friends who draw; abandons ideas midway |
| **Feels** | Excited about ideas, frustrated by lack of art skills, worried about cost, proud when a finished comic is shared |
| **Pains** | No drawing skills; high artist cost; long turnaround; inconsistent character look; difficult panel layout and dialogue placement |
| **Gains** | Instant story-to-comic generation; consistent characters; easy editing of panels and dialogue; downloadable/shareable comic; creative freedom |

## Empathy Map Research Inputs

| Input Source | Collected By | Insight Gained |
|---|---|---|
| Student interviews | Bhuvanesh | Students want quick comics for assignments and hobby stories |
| Educator feedback | V Sanjay Kumar | Teachers need visual, short story content for engagement |
| Creator community review | Gowtham S | Creators want style control and low cost |
| Team discussion and finalisation | Harish M | Focus on simple prompt-to-comic flow |
